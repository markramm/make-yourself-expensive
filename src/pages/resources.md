# Resources

We didn't write everything here from scratch, and you shouldn't stop here either. These are
the guides and organizations the hardening series draws on and points back to — people doing
this work more deeply and more continuously than a single site can.

## Digital security

**[Electronic Frontier Foundation — Surveillance Self-Defense](https://ssd.eff.org)**
The deepest, most current reference for device and communications security. Every guide in
our [Harden](/harden/) series leans on it. If a setting or a legal claim here goes stale,
EFF's version is more likely to be current — check there first.

**[EFF](https://www.eff.org)**
The parent organization: digital rights litigation, policy advocacy, and technical research.
If you want to understand *why* a protection works, or support the people building the next
one, start here.

**[Access Now Digital Security Helpline](https://www.accessnow.org/help/)**
A free, 24/7 helpline for activists, journalists, and civil society if you have specific
reason to believe you're being targeted by government-grade spyware or a sustained digital
attack. help@accessnow.org.

## Data brokers and opt-outs

**[Privacy Rights Clearinghouse](https://privacyrights.org)**
Maintains a data broker registry and consumer-rights resources this project's own
[opt-out dataset](https://github.com/markramm/data-broker-registry) draws on and credits.

**[Yael Grauer — Big Ass Data Broker Opt-Out List](https://github.com/yaelwrites/Big-Ass-Data-Broker-Opt-Out-List)**
An extensively researched, continually updated list of broker opt-out procedures. Also a
direct source for our own dataset — see the credits in
[`LICENSE-DATA`](https://github.com/markramm/data-broker-registry/blob/main/LICENSE-DATA).

**[California DROP tool](https://privacy.ca.gov/)**
If you're a California resident, the state's free Delete Request and Opt-out Platform covers
many brokers in a single request — genuinely faster than going broker-by-broker.

## Hardened operating systems

**[GrapheneOS](https://grapheneos.org)**
A hardened, de-Googled Android build for people at sustained, serious risk — referenced in
the [Android guide](/harden/android/).

**[Tails](https://tails.net)**
An amnesiac operating system you boot from a USB stick for a single sensitive task, then
leave with no trace — referenced in the [Windows guide](/harden/windows/).
